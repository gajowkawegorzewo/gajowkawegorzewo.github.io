A place with a past

A forester's lodge since the 19th century

It all began in the 19th century. This house was built as a forester's lodge — and for decades it was home to hunters who supplied game from the surrounding forests to soldiers and the people of the region.

The walls hold more history than many a museum. Original German inscriptions still survive on the façade, and on the main wall a line of bullet holes — left by Red Army soldiers checking whether anyone was hiding inside. We never painted them over. They are part of this story.

Inside the main building hang photographs of the German families who lived here from the 1880s into the 1930s. Some of the pictures were brought by their descendants, who visited us in recent years — filling in, page by page, the missing chapters of this story.

After 2000 we restored the lodge with respect for what we found. Today it is a place to stay surrounded by the real history of Masuria.

History on your doorstep

📍 30 km · Hitler's HQ

The Wolfsschanze — Hitler's WWII headquarters and site of the 20 July 1944 assassination attempt. We're one of the closest places to stay. Best visited with a guide.

📍 2 km by forest road

The Army High Command bunker complex — far better preserved than the Wolf's Lair. You can go inside and on top of the bunkers. Museum and viewing tower. Reachable on foot through the forest.

🌲 Recommended route through the forest

  1. Navigate to Surwile / Kamień (shown as Surwile on maps; locals say Kamień).
  2. In Surwile/Kamień, turn into the forest at the red „Sołtys" sign.
  3. In the forest: left → left → right — you've arrived!

⚠️ The forest road is passable by any car in summer. In early spring / autumn / winter it can be muddy and stony — not recommended for low sports cars. The road is not plowed in winter.
